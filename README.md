# Madman Photojournalism — Photography Portfolio

A photography portfolio designed as a quiet field journal: travel, nature, and human moments presented with warm paper, forest tones, and room to pause.

## What’s here

- Browse 53 photographs—travel, nature, cities, and people—by subject and reveal more in small batches.
- Open any photograph for a larger view.
- Optional Spotify players for the browsing soundtrack. Audio does not start automatically.
- One Photography gallery includes editorial work and commissioned sessions. Prints remain a separate section with inquiries handled by email.
- Direct inquiry links for photo shoots, print questions, phone, email, and Instagram.
- A restrained notebook feel with a hand-drawn map detail; the photographs remain true to their original digital look.
- A short soundtrack panel follows the About section. Contact details live in the footer.
- The People & sessions and Weddings filters in the Photography gallery contain graduation, expecting-family, couples, and wedding photographs. Older links to `shoots.html` route to that filter.

## Prints and bookings

The site currently routes print questions to email. Shopify checkout is not connected yet. Photo-shoot requests are inquiries; dates and details are confirmed directly by email.

## Files

- `index.html` contains the home page, portfolio, separate booking and print sections, and interactions.
- `shoots.html` preserves older links by routing visitors to People & sessions in the main Photography gallery.
- `assets/portfolio-stories.js` contains the curated photo captions and categories.
- `assets/prints/` contains selected print-feature photographs.
- `assets/field-*.jpg` are web-sized copies for the gallery. Higher-resolution originals are kept separately for print review.
