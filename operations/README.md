# Photography operations

Client session requests: https://photography-hub.andreottati.chatgpt.site/book

Owner mobile dashboard: https://photography-hub.andreottati.chatgpt.site/admin

The booking form saves pending requests. It never confirms a booking or sends a price promise. Client records, replies, shoot information, checklists, payment records and exports require the owner's ChatGPT sign-in. Personal workflow connection is a separate setup step; publishing alone does not establish Gmail/calendar sync. Payments are entered manually; no bank/Zelle credentials are needed. Client-specific photos may link to an owner's iCloud folder rather than duplicate uploads. Pricing remains a private draft until approved.
